Throughout my academic career and ongoing learning, I am dedicated to
maintaining professionalism and upholding ethical standards in all aspects of my
work. As a woman in science from a minority group, I strongly believe that
fostering inclusion is essential for promoting innovation and creativity in
scientific research and collaborations.

Women in Science
======

As a migrant #WomenInScience, our paths are often more complex. I want to inspire
girls everywhere to embrace their diversity, stand proud of their culture, and
bring their unique perspectives into science. I have always been inspired by
learning from incredible #WomenInScience — mentors, colleagues, and collaborators
who have shaped my journey.

Over the past year, I have started a new research path that has brought me
together with a great team in #GenMoz. Together, we are joining forces to help
ensure that malaria does not strike back. I hope our work sparks curiosity in
young girls, and that we can walk alongside them as they pursue science and become
the next generation of researchers working to understand and combat
#InfectiousDiseases.
